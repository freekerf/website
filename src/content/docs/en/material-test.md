---
title: Material test
description: Burn a grid of powers and speeds to find the right settings for a material.
---

A material test burns a grid of small squares: **power rises by row, speed by
column**. Look at the result, pick the square that looks best, and use its power
and speed on your layers.

1. Press **Material test** in the tool rail.
2. Choose **filled** squares (for engraving) or **outlines** (for lines and cuts).
3. Set the power range (from, to, steps; in % of the machine's maximum) and the
   speed range (from, to, steps; mm/min), and the size of a square.
4. Press **Draw the test**.

Each square goes on a layer of its own, named with its power and speed (for
example "40% · 1500 mm/min"), so you can read the values back in the Layers
panel. The labels are engraved in single-stroke text at low power.

Then go to **Produce**, frame and burn it like any job.
